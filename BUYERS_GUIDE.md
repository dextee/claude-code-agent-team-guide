# Is this a useful next step for your business?

VYR works with teams that can name a recurring process, the systems it touches and the person responsible for the outcome. A first conversation determines fit and scope.

**[Discuss your process with VYR →](https://vyrwork.com/contact?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=agent_os_workflows&utm_content=buyers_guide_top)**

## Bring a business problem

Useful starting points include repeated invoice review, enquiries that interrupt the front desk, prospect research that consumes sales time, or onboarding tasks that need constant chasing. [Find a related workflow](docs/workflows/README.md).

Before the conversation, note:

- The current steps and where work slows down.
- The software involved and roughly how often the process runs.
- Common exceptions and decisions that must stay with your team.
- The person who owns the process and your intended timeline.
- The outcome that would make the work worthwhile.

A plain-language description is enough. Use the [VYR contact form](https://vyrwork.com/contact?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=agent_os_workflows&utm_content=buyers_guide_brief) for project context; do not post customer records or credentials in GitHub issues.

## What stays under your control?

Your business owns sensitive decisions and the approvals defined during scoping. The examples identify those boundaries: finance owns payment, recruiters own hiring decisions, clinic staff own clinical questions and marketing owners approve public releases.
